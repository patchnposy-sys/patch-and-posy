Born from memories, stitched by hand

Patch & Posy grew out of memories of my mother's sewing corner. As a child, I watched her sew and embroider, surrounded by bright fabrics, spools of thread and half-finished pieces. Her old Singer hummed through many of those afternoons, and without ever saying so, she taught me to love making things.

Years later, my own PFAFF sits beside that memory. The machines are different, but the feeling is the same: a table covered in colour, with people gathered around it.

That feeling guides my work. I make a dasterkhwan for lunch with cousins, a tablecloth for a Sunday dinner, and a quilt to keep the winter nights warm. I think first about the colours, then the fabric, and then the people who will sit down and eat together.

Patch & Posy is my small studio for patchwork, sewing and thoughtful pieces for the home, including quilts, bedspreads, tablecloths, kitchen pieces and small accessories. Each piece is pieced and finished by hand in good quality cotton. It takes time, and that time matters.

By profession, I am a Software Quality professional. Attention to detail has been part of my working life for many years, and that same patience has carried over into the sewing room.

This little dream is dedicated to the memory of my parents and my mother-in-law, whose love and values still guide the way I work. I am also deeply grateful to my husband, my greatest supporter, who has encouraged me to bring this idea to life.

From my mother's Singer to my PFAFF, the machines have changed, but my love for making things has not.

A table full of colour makes people want to stay a little longer. That is what I sew for. Founder, Patch & Posy

Why handmade, still

In a world of fast production, we have chosen to slow down. Here is what that means in practice.

Made one piece at a time

There are no factory lines. Every quilt and pouch passes through one pair of hands from start to finish, so small imperfections remain. They show that each piece was truly made by hand.

Fabric with a story

We use high quality cotton and linen fabrics, sourced from local mills and pieced the way our mothers taught us. Nothing is wasted, and every piece is made with care.

Comfort over trend

We do not chase seasons. We make pieces that still feel right on your table or bed ten years from now, softening a little more with each year of use.

Stay in touch with Patch & Posy

Receive news about new pieces, restocks, and stories about the women who inspired this work. We only write when we have something worth sharing.
